listed here with its source, licence and the date the licence was checked. An asset that
is not listed here is deleted, not grandfathered. See `CLAUDE.md` §1.3.

## Licences acceptable without asking

- **CC0 / public domain** — no attribution needed, but still recorded here.
- **CC-BY** — allowed; attribution goes in the in-game credits screen *and* this table.

## Needs a decision before use

- Anything **non-commercial**, **no-derivatives**, or with a territory or revenue clause.
- AI-generated 3D from a hosted service: free tiers are frequently CC-BY or
  non-commercial, and terms change. Record the plan and the date, not just the tool name.
